title: The assistant
description: The workspace assistant — opening it, what it can read, which actions wait for your confirmation, how roles apply, flow proposals, and what happens without the model.
---

The assistant is a chat panel for the whole workspace. Ask what needs attention, find orders,
check stock, read a flow, or have it decide an approval, deliver an event, cancel an order, publish
a flow or run the stock review, or (as an admin) draft a plugin. It answers from live data through tools, and anything that changes
something stops for your confirmation first.

One assistant serves every page.

## Opening it

- The **Assistant** button at the top of the page, or <kbd>⌘J</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd>) anywhere,
  which also closes it. On wide screens the panel reopens on your next visit if you left it open.
- The **Ask** button on an order page or in the flow editor opens the panel about that order or flow.
- Changing something from a page. See [Edits from a page](#edits-from-a-page).

## Edits from a page

Changes you make on a page go through the assistant. The page's button doesn't change anything
itself. Instead, the assistant runs the tool for exactly what you pressed, with no model involved,
so these edits work even when the model is unavailable. Each edit stays in your conversation as a
line.

- **An action** stops at its confirm card in the panel. The panel opens if it was closed. This
  covers adding, pausing or resuming a promotion, verifying or revoking a tax certificate,
  publishing the catalog, publishing or test-running a flow, deciding approvals (one or a batch), cancelling an order, delivering an event, starting
  an order from **New order** (the form checks its fields first), restarting an order,
  submitting or cancelling a cart and running the stock review. The page waits until you answer, and declining leaves everything as it
  was.
- **Anything else** runs at once and doesn't open the panel. This covers running a demo scenario,
  which opens its order, and changing a cart's lines,
  promo codes, ship-to and shipping option, and locking or unlocking it. The page updates as usual.

These edits are checked like any other tool call: your role, the live order or cart, and the same
audit trail (`via` the assistant, marked `direct` so a button press reads apart from a model's
proposal). The conversation keeps only what each line needs: an action keeps what its card states,
and any other edit keeps only its ids and what it did. Cart text, addresses and names stay on the
page. A buyer on an external cart has no assistant, so their own
changes go straight to the cart.

Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line; on a phone or tablet Return starts a new line and the
send button sends. **Stop** ends a reply in progress. A message written while a reply is still
streaming is held, shown above the prompt where you can edit or remove it, and sent when the reply
ends. A question you send rises to the top of the panel and its reply fills in below it; a long
reply is followed as it grows. When you scroll up during a reply, the panel stops following it; the
arrow at the bottom takes you back to the latest. Reopening the panel shows your last question.

### Copying, editing and asking again

Each message has actions beside or under it (with a mouse or trackpad, point at the message to
show them):

- **Copy** copies a message's text.
- **Edit** on one of your messages opens it for changes. **Send again** replaces that message, and
  every reply after it, with an answer to the new text. Escape cancels.
- **Answer again** on the latest reply asks for a new answer to the same question, in place of the
  old one.

**New conversation** (in the panel's **⋯** menu) asks first, then deletes the conversation;
actions you confirmed stay done and stay in the audit log.

Editing and asking again never reach back past an action the assistant prepared for you to
confirm: whether you confirmed it or not, it stays in the conversation as it happened, so those
messages offer only **Copy**.

If a reply fails, the panel says so under the conversation with **Try again**, which asks for the
reply again.

### The context chip

The chip beside the send button says what "this" means: the order or flow open on the page, or
**Everything** when you are elsewhere. Open the chip to switch between the page and the whole
workspace. The page's context travels with each message you send. In the flow editor it includes
the graph on screen, unsaved changes included.

### The attention line

The top of the panel always shows what is waiting on people, refreshed while the panel is open and
after each reply, with links to each item:

- open approvals, oldest first;
- orders that failed or rolled back in the last 3 days;
- orders that have waited on a business event for over a day.

## What it can read

Every role can read. The tools are `needs_attention`, `find_orders` (by status, model, channel, customer,
text, total, age, and the step each one waits at or stopped at), `get_order` (status, the step it
waits at and since when, steps run, recent activity),
`assess_order` (typed questions about one order, answered with a probability for every option; up to 4 images),
`find_products` (stock, reserved, available, reorder points), `list_carts` and `get_cart` (a
[cart's](/guides/carts/) lines with their ids, its price with shipping and tax, promotion codes,
its ship-to and the shipping options on offer, the problems its price
found, and why it can't be submitted yet), `list_flows` and `get_flow` (a saved
draft with its validation problems). Text, customer and SKU searches match `%` and `_` literally, not as LIKE wildcards. Reads run without asking, and each shows as a line in the
conversation: what it is doing while the reply streams, then what it found. An order's line names
the step it waits at or stopped at, and opens that step on the order's graph; answers about an order
say the same. A line for one cart, flow or assessment links to it too, and a reply that opened
several of them ends with a **Used** line that lists each one. A read that failed, or that a reply ended before it finished, says so and shows why.
When the model thinks before it answers, a **Thought** line above its reads opens to show that
thinking. Now and then the model stops after thinking and reading without answering; the reply then
ends with **Stopped without an answer**, and **Answer again** asks once more.

## Actions that ask first

These tools never run on the model's say-so. The panel shows a confirm card for each; nothing is
sent until you press the confirm button, and declining leaves everything as it was.

| Action | Needs | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `decide_approval` | `approver` | Approves or rejects the approval an order waits on, with an optional note |
| `decide_approvals` | `approver` | Approves or rejects up to 25 open approvals on one card, with one note; each is decided on its own, and one that can't be is skipped with the reason |
| `deliver_event` | `operator` | Delivers a business event to the order waiting for it, optionally with up to 20 flat fields it carries, each value at most 120 characters and shown quoted on the card. The fields a source writes itself (`by`, `event`, `at`, `outcome`, `source`, `provider`, `invoiceId`, `paymentIntentId`, `note`) are refused |
| `cancel_order` | `operator` | Cancels the order and undoes what it did, as [Cancelling](/guides/approvals-and-events/#cancelling) |
| `submit_cart` | `operator` | Submits a priced cart as an order of its flow, as [Submit as order](/guides/carts/#locking-submitting-and-cancelling) |
| `publish_flow` | `operator` | Publishes the flow's saved draft as the next version |
| `run_stock_review` | `operator` | Starts the stock review now |
| `start_order` | `operator` | Starts an order on a published flow: its lines, and optionally a customer or guest contact, fulfilment, channel, currency, title and document text. The card lists everything the order acts on: its lines, the customer or the guest's email (where the flow's messages go), fulfilment, and any title, currency, channel or document text (its length and opening). It is blocked for an unpublished flow, an unknown SKU or customer, both a customer and a guest, a pickup or carry-out without a store, or a currency with no rate |
| `restart_order` | `operator` | Starts a fresh order like an existing one, on its flow's current version, without demo failure switches; the old order stays as it is |
| `cancel_cart` | `operator` | Cancels an open or locked cart; nothing is ordered |
| `create_promotion` | `operator` | Adds a promotion code, active at once: a percentage or amount off, or free shipping, optionally limited by subtotal, SKUs, customer, end date and uses |
| `set_promotion_status` | `operator` | Pauses a promotion code or reactivates it |
| `verify_tax_certificate` | `operator` | Verifies a customer's tax exemption certificate, so their new orders are exempt in its regions. With approval separation on, whoever added it can't |
| `revoke_tax_certificate` | `operator` | Revokes a customer's tax exemption certificate, so their new orders are taxed again |

These four change what buyers pay or owe in tax, so a person always confirms them: on the panel's
card, through an MCP confirmation, or with `{ "confirm": true }` in an A2A chat. They aren't typed
A2A skills. Only a signed-in person verifies a certificate, never an API token.
| `set_member_role` | `admin` | Changes a person's role. Lowering it revokes their API tokens above the new role; making someone an admin needs a step-up |
| `set_approval_separation` | `admin` | Sets whether people may approve their own orders and verify certificates they added |
| `set_plugin_enabled` | `admin` | Turns an installed plugin on or off (a person confirms it's them; an admin token may turn one off) |
| `approve_plugin` | `admin` | Installs an uploaded or drafted plugin version; needs a step-up |
| `rollback_plugin` | `admin` | Runs an earlier version of a plugin again; needs a step-up |
| `uninstall_plugin` | `admin` | Uninstalls a plugin installed at runtime; needs a step-up |
| `publish_catalog` | `operator` | Publishes up to 500 products' staged edits to the live catalog, each product all or none. The card lists each product and its staged fields, and is held to them |
| `test_flow` | `operator` | Publishes a flow's saved draft when it changed since the live version, then starts a test order with a demo input; the card says which |

An order started from a card is started once for that card: if the same confirm is run again (say,
after the assistant restarted mid-run), it finds the order it already started. Once a card is
answered, the conversation keeps only its ids, so a guest buyer's contact isn't stored there. A
page's card left unanswered for a day is dropped as declined, and only its ids are kept.

Flows have writes that run without a card, since a draft runs only once published: `create_flow`
(a model's template, a blank start, or drafted from a description), `duplicate_flow` and
`archive_flow` (archive or restore a draft that was never published). `save_flow_draft` is the
editor's **Save**, and only the editor calls it, since it overwrites the draft. The assistant
proposes changes with `propose_flow_change` instead, which you apply in the editor.

A card that needs a step-up asks you to confirm it's you before it confirms: a code from your
authenticator app, your password, or signing in again with single sign-on. The engine reads when
your session last did that from the session itself and allows ten minutes. Each socket holds its own session, so a step-up on one device never confirms a card on another.
A step-up with single sign-on lasts ten minutes from the sign-in at your provider and isn't
extended. A connected app or API token has no session, so it can't make those changes. Changing a
role and the approvals setting are a signed-in person's alone, in the app: they aren't offered
over MCP or A2A. Running a plugin draft's test cases (`run_plugin_cases`) runs from
the Plugins page only.

The Catalog page's staging, accepting and dropping (`stage_product_edits`, `accept_product_drafts`,
`discard_product_edits`) run only from its buttons, since each overwrites or drops what waits to
publish. The assistant drafts with `draft_product_content`, and a person accepts.

`start_scenario` (`operator`) runs one of the demo scenarios by its id, without a card, only where
the demo data is loaded, and only from its button on the dashboard or the scenarios page. The model
is never offered it, and MCP and A2A don't list it.

The card is not the model's summary. It is resolved against live data when it appears, and states
the consequence: for a decision, the order, its total and which step it goes on to; for a cancel,
the effects it will undo and the reason; for a cart, its lines, total, flow and customer, that its
flow prices the order again when it runs, where it ships, and that the cart ends; for a publish, what changed since the live version and
that unsaved editor changes are not included; for the stock review, which SKUs are at or below
their reorder point. When the action can't go ahead (already decided, nothing waiting for that
event, the order has finished, you started the order and the workspace separates approvals, the cart isn't priced or has a problem, the draft has errors, nothing to publish, or your role is too low)
the card says why and offers no confirm.

While a card waits for your answer, a bar above the prompt says so (**Waiting for you**) and
**Show** brings the card back into view, however far the conversation has moved on. You can keep
asking meanwhile. The conversation is the same on every device you're signed in on, so a card can
be answered from any of them, once. If the same action is prepared again, only the newest card asks;
the older one says it was replaced. Escape never declines a card: declining is always **Not now**.

Once answered, a card becomes one line among the reads around it: what was done (linking to it),
that you declined and it was left as it was, or that it didn't go through, which opens to show why.

Confirming runs the action as you, after the engine re-checks your role and the order or flow
again. The state can have changed since the card appeared, in which case the action is refused with
the reason. A cart is held to the card exactly: if it changed at all after the card first appeared, the
submit is refused and nothing is ordered, and the same card opened again (in another tab, say)
says so instead of offering a confirm; ask again for a new card. The audit trail names the actor as `<email> via assistant`: that string is the approval's
`by`, or ends the cancel reason. A confirmed action is also an [audit log](/guides/team-and-roles/#audit-log) event (`via` the assistant, with the `tool`); read-only tools are not logged.

## Roles

The assistant works within your role (`viewer`, `approver`, `operator`, `admin`, in order; only `draft_plugin` needs `admin`). A tool your role can't use is
not offered to the model, and is refused if it is called anyway; the assistant says which role can.
Every role can use the read tools; plugin drafts are for admins. The role is re-read from your session when a tool runs, so a
role change, a sign-out or a disabled account applies to an open panel at once. See
[Team and roles](/guides/team-and-roles/).

## Flow changes

Ask for a change to a flow ("add a fraud check after credit"). The `propose_flow_change` tool
(`operator`) drafts a whole new graph with the same copilot that creates flows, validates it, and
returns the explanation, a count of what it adds, removes, edits and rewires, and any problems. It
never saves anything. **Review in editor** opens the flow's editor with the proposal, where
accepting it is one undoable edit that you can still save and publish. In the editor the draft
builds on the graph on screen. A request that changes nothing says so, with no card.

Creating a flow from a description still happens on the Flows page.

## Carts

Operators can have the assistant build a [cart](/guides/carts/): `create_cart` opens one (a title,
a published flow that prices orders, and optionally a customer) and `change_cart` adds SKUs, sets
or removes lines, clears it, adds or removes promotion codes, sets or clears the ship-to address,
chooses a shipping option, locks, unlocks or renames it, a few
changes at a time, in order. Neither asks first, because nothing is ordered until the cart is
submitted, and each change is made as you, exactly as on the cart's page: a locked or ended cart
refuses it, and the reply says how many changes went in before one was refused. The cart is
priced again by its flow after each change. Submitting is `submit_cart`, behind a confirm card.
The assistant reaches carts your team opens; a cart a customer opened from their own purchasing
system stays on its own page. It never reads a cart's chat or files.

## Plugin drafts

An admin can ask for a business rule ("hold orders over $5,000 shipping outside the US") and the
`draft_plugin` tool (`admin`) drafts a [plugin](/guides/installing-plugins/#drafts-from-the-assistant)
of rule steps with test cases. It runs directly, with no confirm card, because nothing it writes
runs. The card in the panel shows the plugin's name and version, the steps it adds, and how many
test cases pass, with a **Review** button that opens the Plugins page. Approving happens only there.

The draft sees only an order's commercial facts, never names, emails or addresses, and can't reach
outside hosts, secrets or storage. To revise a draft, ask again naming the plugin; the assistant
drafts its next version. If the workspace hasn't had this turned on by OrderLoop, the assistant says
it can't.

## Product content

Operators can have the assistant write a catalog product's missing text: "draft the French
description for product TEE-100", or just "fill in what TEE-100 is missing". The
`draft_product_content` tool (`operator`) writes each title, description, text attribute and image
alt text the product lacks in a locale, from what it says in its other locales, or the fields you
name, and saves them as drafts on the product. It skips text that already has a draft or an edit
waiting, and writes up to 20 fields a request, titles and descriptions first; ask again for the
rest. It keeps product names, brands and numbers as they
are and adds nothing the product doesn't say; a field it can't write from the product is listed as
not drafted. It runs directly, with no confirm card, because nothing goes live until someone accepts
a draft and publishes it (see [Editing content](/guides/product-catalog/#editing-content)).

## Daily allowance

Each person can use up to 1,000,000 model tokens a day in the assistant, counted from midnight UTC.
When it is spent, the assistant says "You've used today's assistant allowance. It resets at
midnight UTC; your OrderLoop contact can raise it." and answers again the next day. A
[plugin draft](#plugin-drafts) or [product draft](#product-content) counts against the allowance of
the person who asked for it, and is refused once it is spent. Answers from the fixed rules below cost nothing. Your OrderLoop administrator can raise the limit or remove it.
MCP clients bring their own model, so they aren't counted.

## Without the model

The assistant answers with `@cf/moonshotai/kimi-k2.6` by default (see
[AI nodes and the assistant](/guides/ai/)). If that model fails before it says anything, a smaller,
faster model, `@cf/zai-org/glm-4.7-flash`, takes the turn; its replies aren't labelled differently.
When neither answers (AI is unavailable, both models error, or nothing useful arrives within 20
seconds), a deterministic rule-based model takes the turn instead. It calls the same tools through
the same confirm cards.

A reply from the fallback is labelled **Answered without the language model, from fixed rules**.
It understands plain requests ("what needs attention", "add a risk score after
credit check", "fill in what product TEE-100 is missing"), not open questions.

## Over MCP

The same tools are served to MCP clients such as Claude, Claude Code and Cursor at `/mcp`, with the
same roles. `draft_plugin` needs `admin`, which a connected app is never granted, so only an admin [API token](/guides/team-and-roles/#api-tokens) reaches it. There is no confirm card: an action's first call changes nothing. A client that can
ask you shows the preview and acts only once you agree; any other client gets a preview and a
one-time code, and the action runs only when it calls again with the code (see
[Actions wait for the person](/reference/mcp/#actions-wait-for-the-person)). The
engine still re-checks the role and the state before it acts. Writes are recorded as
`<email> via MCP`. See [MCP server](/reference/mcp/). Other agents reach the same tools over the
Agent2Agent protocol at `/a2a`, as skills; see [A2A endpoint](/reference/a2a/).

## Privacy

Each person has one assistant and one conversation, tied to their signed-in account, so nobody can
open another person's thread. The conversation keeps the last 200 messages. **Conversation options**
in the panel clears it. A reply that is in progress when the tab closes keeps streaming and is there
when you come back.
Orders, flows and approvals are read from the same data the dashboard shows, so the assistant tells
you nothing your role couldn't see.
