OrderLoop seeds at least one flow per [commerce model](/guides/commerce-models/). Each is an ordinary flow: open it in the editor,
change it and publish a new version.

## Seeding

OrderLoop publishes the built-in flows the first time they are needed: when flows are listed or
opened, when an order starts, and before the stock review starts a replenishment order. When a
built-in flow changes in a new OrderLoop release, the new version is published over the flow's
draft and over anything you published from the editor since, so keep a customised copy as its own
flow. A built-in flow that matches an earlier version is never published again, and orders already
running keep the version they pinned.

## Scenarios

The dashboard's **Demo scenarios** start an order on a built-in flow with a canned input. Each
exercises a different path:

| Scenario | Flow | Path |
|---|---|---|
| Guest checkout | `tpl-storefront-order` | A `b2c` web order with `contact` and no customer: card checkout, and the shipping and return emails go to the contact address |
| B2B PO by email | `tpl-b2b-order` | AI extracts lines from an email, contract pricing, the credit check passes, the routine assessment is unsure (without the model it always answers 0.5), so the agent router triages and auto-approves; ships, waits for payment |
| B2B over credit limit | `tpl-b2b-order` | `cus_contoso` is near its limit, so the credit check routes to a person |
| B2B out of stock | `tpl-b2b-order` | `meta.simulate: "out_of_stock"`: the reservation comes up short and the order fails and rolls back |
| Procurement request | `tpl-purchase-request` | Eight laptops: AI classifies spend, manager then finance approve, PO, supplier ack, 3-way match |
| Reorder (stock review) | `tpl-reorder` | The forecast finds SKUs below their reorder point and drafts a replenishment PO |
| Subscription | `tpl-subscription` | Authorize, then a loop of wait → charge → ship |
| Subscription, card fails | `tpl-subscription` | `meta.simulate: "charge_fail"`: the renewal and its retry both fail and the subscription pauses |
| Storefront order in euros | `tpl-storefront-order` | The retail checkout in `EUR`: USD prices convert at the stored rate, and the order, capture and webhooks are in euros |
| Marketplace order, two sellers | `tpl-marketplace-order` | `HP-200` from a seller that auto-accepts and `KET-01` from one that is asked: the order waits for Kettle & Co, then ships what was accepted and schedules each seller's payout net of commission. See [Marketplace](/guides/marketplace/) |
| Buy online, pick up in store | `tpl-omnichannel-order` | A monitor held at the SoHo store with a pickup code; the order waits for `pickup.collected`, then hands it over and captures payment |
| Ship from store, with an e-book | `tpl-omnichannel-order` | The warehouse has no tees, so they ship from the SoHo store, the nearest to the New York ship-to (which carries coordinates); the e-book is delivered as a code, and payment is captured once both parts are done |
| Split shipment, nearest store | `tpl-omnichannel-order` | Eight monitors and three tees to San Francisco: no one place has both, so the monitors ship from the warehouse and the tees from the Mission store, as two shipments |
| Till sale with a gift card | `tpl-pos-sale` | A tee and a gift card at the Mission store: card present, the tee leaves store stock, the gift card gets a code, and the receipt is emailed |
| Storefront order, returned | `tpl-storefront-order` | Ships and captures, then a return: RMA label, restock, refund. A `return.requested` payload with `lines: [{ sku, qty }]` restocks only those lines; `amount` sets a partial refund |

### Simulating failures

The built-in commerce steps read `order.meta.simulate`, so any order (from the API too) can take a
failure path:

| Value | Effect |
|---|---|
| `out_of_stock` | Reserve inventory reports every line short |
| `payment_decline` | Authorize payment takes `declined` |
| `charge_fail` | Charge takes `failed` |
| `three_way_mismatch` | 3-way match sees an invoice 10% over the PO and takes `mismatch` |

Restarting an order from its page drops `meta.simulate`, so the retry runs for real.

### Stock review

The stock review runs daily at 06:00 UTC. It scans for
SKUs at or below their reorder point and, if there are any, starts one order on `tpl-reorder`
titled `Replenish <skus>`. **Run stock review now** on the back office page starts it on demand.
